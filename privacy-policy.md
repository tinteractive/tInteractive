# Privacy Policy — Weather - Live Forecast

*Last updated: June 2, 2026*

## Information We Collect

Weather - Live Forecast requests access to your device's location (GPS) to display local weather information. Location data is used exclusively on your device and is sent only to the following third-party services to retrieve weather and location data:

- **Open-Meteo** (open-meteo.com) — weather forecast data
- **Nominatim / OpenStreetMap** (openstreetmap.org) — reverse geocoding (converting GPS coordinates to a place name)

We do not collect, store, or share your personal data on our own servers.

## In-App Purchases

The app offers a one-time in-app purchase (Pro upgrade) processed by Google Play Billing. We do not have access to your payment information.

## Third-Party Services

This app uses Google Play Services (location and billing). Please refer to [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) for more information.
