# Privacy Policy — Calendar Widget

*Last updated: June 8, 2026*

## Information We Collect

Calendar Widget requests access to your device's calendar data to display your events and agenda in the app and on your home screen widgets.

Calendar information is processed locally on your device. We do not collect, store, or transmit your calendar events to our own servers.

If you customize widget settings such as layout, colors, selected calendars, display preferences, or weather options, those settings are stored locally on your device only.

## Location and Weather Information

If you enable weather information for a widget, the app may request access to your device's location (GPS) in order to provide local weather data.

Your location is used only to retrieve relevant weather information and, if applicable, a place name for display. Location data is not stored on our servers.

To provide weather features, the app may send your location data to the following third-party services:

- **Open-Meteo** ([open-meteo.com](https://open-meteo.com/)) — weather forecast data

If you do not enable weather features, the app does not need to access your location for this purpose.

## In-App Purchases

The app offers a one-time in-app purchase (Premium upgrade) processed by Google Play Billing. We do not have access to your payment card details or other payment information.

Google may process purchase-related data in accordance with its own policies. Please refer to [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and [Google Payments Privacy Notice](https://payments.google.com/legaldocument?family=0.privacynotice&hl=en-GB) for more information.

## Third-Party Services

This app may use the following third-party services to provide certain functionality:

- **Google Play Services** — app distribution and related platform services
- **Google Play Billing** — in-app purchases
- **Open-Meteo** — weather forecast data

No calendar data is shared with us or stored on our servers.

## Data Sharing

We do not sell, share, or transfer your personal data to third parties, except where necessary to provide app functionality through the services listed above.
