# Privacy Policy — Weather - Live Forecast

*Last updated: June 8, 2026*

## Information We Collect

Weather - Live Forecast requests access to your device's location (GPS) to display local weather information. Location data is used exclusively on your device and is sent only to the following third-party services to retrieve weather and location data:

- **Open-Meteo** (open-meteo.com) — weather forecast data
- **Nominatim / OpenStreetMap** (openstreetmap.org) — reverse geocoding (converting GPS coordinates to a place name)

If you customize widget settings such as layout, colors, selected locations, or display preferences, those settings are stored locally on your device only. We do not collect, store, or share your personal data on our own servers.

## In-App Purchases

The app offers a one-time in-app purchase (Premium upgrade) processed by Google Play Billing. We do not have access to your payment card details or other payment information.

Google may process purchase-related data in accordance with its own policies. Please refer to [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and [Google Payments Privacy Notice](https://payments.google.com/legaldocument?family=0.privacynotice&hl=en-GB) for more information.

## Third-Party Services

This app may use the following third-party services to provide certain functionality:

- **Google Play Services** — app distribution and related platform services
- **Google Play Billing** — in-app purchases
- **Open-Meteo** — weather forecast data
- **Nominatim / OpenStreetMap** — reverse geocoding

No data is shared with us or stored on our servers.

## Data Sharing

We do not sell, share, or transfer your personal data to third parties, except where necessary to provide app functionality through the services listed above.
